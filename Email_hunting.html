Paste the raw email headers, or upload a full .eml file (which also lets the tool inspect links & attachments).

How this works & its limits. All parsing runs locally in your browser — no email content is sent anywhere. The verdict is a heuristic risk score based on authentication results, sender alignment, link patterns, and attachment types. It is a triage aid, not a definitive ruling: sophisticated phishing can pass these checks, and legitimate mail can occasionally trip them. This tool does not perform live URL reputation lookups or sandbox attachments — escalate anything uncertain to your security team.
